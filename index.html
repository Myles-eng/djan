About

I create because ideas can change what people understand, trust and do.

I am Geoffrey Afari-Djan, a Ghanaian Brand & Digital Marketing Professional with more than eight years of experience across branding, digital content, social media strategy, film and creative leadership.

My work sits between strategy and execution. I enjoy shaping the concept, building the system and bringing the idea to life through design, film, photography, digital content or physical brand experiences.

Over four and a half years at Zipline, my responsibilities expanded from creative execution into regional brand and content leadership across Ghana, Nigeria, Rwanda and Côte d'Ivoire. I have developed regional content strategies, launched new social channels, produced more than 100 professional marketing assets, supported major funding narratives and coordinated multi-country branding and production programmes.

I hold a Master's degree in Digital & Strategic Marketing Management and I am pursuing a professional certificate in Brand Management. My creative foundation includes graphic design, filmmaking, photography, motion graphics and documentary storytelling.

I believe creativity should do more than decorate. It should clarify a problem, create connection and move an organization forward.
